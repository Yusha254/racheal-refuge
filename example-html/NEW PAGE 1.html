About Rachel's Refuge

Rachel's Refuge is a basketball initiative created to provide a safe, supportive and inspiring environment where girls can discover their talents and develop their basketball skills. We believe that basketball is more than just a game. It teaches discipline, teamwork, confidence, leadership and determination.

Our Programs

Our programs are designed to support young players at different stages of their basketball journey.

🏀

Basketball Training

Professional basketball training focusing on shooting, passing, dribbling, defense and teamwork.

👩‍🦰

Girls Empowerment

Programs designed to build confidence, leadership skills and self-belief among young girls.

🏆

Competitions

Friendly matches and tournaments that give players an opportunity to showcase their abilities.

⭐

Talent Development

Identifying and supporting talented players so they can achieve their basketball goals.

Our Progress

Rachel's Refuge continues to grow by reaching more young players, organizing basketball activities and creating opportunities for girls to participate in sports. Our goal is to build a strong basketball community where every girl feels valued, supported and inspired to succeed.
