Our Programs

Building confidence, leadership and opportunity for girls through basketball, mentorship, education and community engagement.

Featured Program

Our upcoming school visitation program will connect Rachel's Refuge with schools and students through basketball and mentorship.

🏫 School Visitation Week

MAY 10 – MAY 16, 2027

Rachel's Refuge will conduct a week-long school visitation program from Monday, May 10, 2027 through Sunday, May 16, 2027.

The visitation program is designed to introduce students to the opportunities available through basketball, mentorship, education and leadership.

During the visits, the Rachel's Refuge team will interact with students, coaches, teachers and school communities while encouraging girls to believe in their abilities and pursue their goals.

What We Do

🏀

Basketball Development

Girls receive opportunities to develop basketball fundamentals, teamwork, discipline and confidence on and off the court.

💜

Girls Mentorship

Mentorship sessions encourage young girls to develop confidence, positive values, personal responsibility and strong relationships.

📚

School Outreach

We visit schools to connect with students, share experiences and encourage girls to pursue education, sports and their dreams.

⭐

Leadership Development

Participants are encouraged to become leaders, role models and positive influences within their schools and communities.

🤝

Community Engagement

Rachel's Refuge works to build relationships with schools, families, coaches and community partners.

🌟

Girls Empowerment

Our programs encourage girls to recognize their potential and understand that their circumstances do not have to determine their future.

Program Objectives

01. Build Confidence

Help girls develop confidence in their abilities both on and off the basketball court.

02. Encourage Education

Encourage students to value education and understand its importance in achieving their future goals.

03. Develop Leadership

Provide opportunities for girls to develop leadership skills and become positive role models.

04. Promote Teamwork

Use basketball to teach communication, cooperation, discipline and respect.
